About

Car Wash & Car Care Center is a neighborhood hand car wash on Chamkar Doung Street, built around straightforward service and a clean, careful finish. We wash and detail every vehicle by hand — from daily runabouts to work trucks — because a properly cared-for car should be within easy reach, not a luxury. Stop by, or give us a call.
